Our Mission

Pack Forever Foundation exists to ensure Colorado State University Pueblo student-athlete alumni have access to a strong, lifelong community of support. We provide resources, connections, and assistance to strengthen the Pack as challenges arise.

2026 Objectives

Awareness

Expanding recognition of the foundation and increasing visibility within the community.

Education

Clearly communicating our mission, purpose, and the impact we aim to make.

Support

Building the financial and community backing needed to sustain and grow our efforts.

Meet the Board

Pack Forever Foundation is led by former CSU Pueblo student-athletes who are committed to giving back to the community that shaped them.
